WHO WE ARE

Practical solutions for industry.

East Coast Instruments has been operating in the instrumentation industry since 1984. Based in Richards Bay and serving customers across South Africa, we provide instrumentation products, technical knowledge and service to support industrial operations.

Our business supplies instrumentation, hydraulics, pneumatics, electronics, laboratory and analytical products, as well as gas detection solutions.

We work with customers across different industries and focus on providing practical solutions that support productivity, reliability and efficient operations.
